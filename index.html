Chat in Morse code.用摩尔斯电码聊天。

DitMesh is a serverless Morse messenger over the Tox peer-to-peer network. Key with a straight key or iambic paddle, by touch or physical keyboard, in direct conversations and group nets.DitMesh 是基于 Tox 点对点网络的无服务器摩尔斯电码聊天工具。用触摸或实体键盘操作直键、双桨，在单聊和群组中发报。

How a message travels一条消息如何传递

  1. 1

    You key it你拍发

    Tap, squeeze or press. DitMesh decodes your marks while keeping their rhythm.点按、按压或敲击。DitMesh 在保留节奏的同时解码你的拍发。

  2. 2

    Tox carries itTox 传输

    Plain text and timing travel end-to-end encrypted between reachable peers.文字与时序经端到端加密，在可达的对等设备之间传输。

  3. 3

    They hear it对方收听

    Their player follows their preferred speed, spacing and audio or haptic output.对方播放器按其设定的速度、间距和音频/触觉方式播放。

FAQ

Do I need an account?需要注册账号吗？

No. A Tox identity is generated on your device. Back it up from Me because there is no central account provider to recover it.不需要。Tox 身份在设备本地生成。请在“我的”中备份，因为没有中心账号服务可以帮你恢复。

Can I message someone who is offline?对方离线时能发消息吗？

The message stays in the local send queue and continues when both peers are running and reachable. Keep DitMesh running to receive messages.消息会留在本地待发送队列，双方运行且网络可达时继续发送。请保持 DitMesh 运行以接收消息。

Is my message stored on a server?消息会存储在服务器上吗？

No messaging or push server is involved. Tox carries encrypted data peer to peer; history and backups stay on your devices.不会。没有聊天或推送服务器，Tox 点对点传输加密数据；历史记录和备份保留在你的设备上。

What is the relationship with MorseCQ?DitMesh 与 MorseCQ 有什么关系？

DitMesh is the live messenger. For offline courses, structured training and statistics, use MorseCQ.DitMesh 是实时聊天工具。离线课程、系统训练与统计功能请使用 MorseCQ。
